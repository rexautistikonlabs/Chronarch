# THREATS.md — Threat Model

This document is the adversary's-eye view of Chronarch, a decentralized autonomous cognitive organism (DACO — explicitly not a claim of consciousness or qualia; PoQ does not prove subjective experience, G10). It names who attacks the organism, what each attacker tries, and why every named attack is either rejected outright, bounded by economics and prestress, or — when it lands — metabolized into a permanent scar instead of a silent loss. It also does something most threat models skip: it lists, openly, what the MVP does **not** yet defend, and exactly where residual trust still lives. The kernel modules most exercised here are `K8_immune_gym_catalog`, `K11_witness_rule`, and `K18_reject_list`; every defense claimed below is either a schema rejection, a Genesis Law, a nervous-system interface ([NERVOUS.md](NERVOUS.md)), or a rehearsed gym case ([GYM.md](GYM.md)) — never an unstated assumption of good behavior.

Status: v0 draft — sim/MVP values are FROZEN-MVP; changing them post-genesis is a MAJOR change (G14).

> Verbatim from `SLOGANS["security"]`:
>
> **"Tampering is detectable, expensive, incomplete, and metabolized into a scar."**

That sentence is the whole security posture. Chronarch does not promise that attacks never land. It promises four bounded properties: tampering is **detectable** (hash walks, challenges, witness attestations), **expensive** (bonds, deposits, slashing, real disk), **incomplete** (no admin key, no helm override, no single lever that reaches everything — G17), and **metabolized** (every wound seals a Scar that can never be pruned — G5). The [GYM.md](GYM.md) catalog re-proves all four on schedule, against Chronarch targets only (G12).

## 1. Attacker classes

Every threat in Section 2 is mounted by one or more of these adversaries. No class is assumed honest; each is bounded by structure, not trust.

| Class | Who they are | Standing bound |
|---|---|---|
| **External adversary** | No stake, no bond, no seat. Submits transactions, forges objects, probes the mempool (I6). | Everything they submit passes admission screening: closed K2 schemas, the K18 `REJECT_LIST` / `FORBIDDEN_KEY_TOKENS`, and challenge-before-belief (G6). They can spend, but they cannot sign as anyone bonded — so they can be rejected but rarely slashed. |
| **Byzantine farmer / validator** | Holds real plots and/or a full node; may equivocate, withhold pins, or answer plot challenges dishonestly. | Plots prove space and nothing else — they never store rings, embeddings, or weights, so a byzantine farmer corrupts availability, not truth. Dishonesty is felt at I2/I3 as a nervous event, and both lanes (PLOT and CAMBIUM/CAS) are paid work that stops paying: `farmer_plot_share = 3500` bps and `pin_share = 1500` bps route only to honest proofs and served pins ([TOKEN.md](TOKEN.md)). |
| **Malicious councilor(s), below threshold** | Fewer than yes bond weight ≥ 2/3 of eligible bond weight, or no strict majority of eligible seats. | Cannot approve anything: approval requires **both** thresholds against eligible totals, so abstention counts against them ([COUNCIL.md](COUNCIL.md), K14). Their bonds (`MIN_COUNCIL_BOND_CHRONONS` = 1000 Chronos each) remain slashable for illegal votes (G16). |
| **Malicious councilor(s), above threshold** | A colluding supermajority that clears both thresholds. | Even a supermajority cannot ratify a proposal violating G1–G13 — such a vote is invalid **and slashable** (G16, T4); cannot flip a Challenge (G2, T8); cannot touch the covenant without a hard fork (G7); and every approval waits `ACTIVATION_DELAY_SLOTS = 32` before activating, a public reaction window. What remains — legal-but-bad proposals — is the residual governance risk, bounded by bonds at stake and named honestly in Section 4. |
| **Compromised helm (Chronarch itself misbehaving)** | The helm cognition goes wrong — bad training, bad inputs, or outright capture. | Chronarch proposes; it may never enact major change alone (G15). No helm private key exists. Its authored outputs are inert until activation (G4), its M-class actions require Proposal + Ballot (G14), and a helm that tries to act anyway hits T3: the action stays inert. The organism survives its own mind being wrong — that is the point of the division of labor. |
| **Wealthy briber** | Unlimited Chronos, willing to buy outcomes. | "Chronos is blood, not conscience" (G2, `COVENANT_SEED`). Chronos buys stake weight, liquidity, and pins — never judgment: Challenge outcomes and Ballot legality are ruled by deterministic core logic that has no fee input (T8, T4). Even the salience overlay money can influence is clamped to retrieval **ranking only** (`SALIENCE_CLAMP_MIN_BPS = 2500` to `SALIENCE_CLAMP_MAX_BPS = 40000`), never to Challenge outcomes or Ballot validity. |
| **Griefing challenger** | Spams challenges to burn honest nodes' compute and reputation. | Challenges are deterministic replay/retrieval (G6): honest identities pass them cheaply and mechanically, so griefing buys noise, not verdicts. Contained per the `griefing_challenge` gym oracle; any per-challenge bond or rate-limit value is TBD (requires Proposal + Ballot). |
| **Eclipse adversary** | Isolates a node or captures head witnesses to feed a victim a false present. | The witness rule (K11) requires `WITNESS_K = 3` of `WITNESS_N = 5` head witnesses; conflicting attestations are detectable and scar at I7, and witness bonds are slashable. Full 3-of-5 capture is the MVP's named residual trust surface — see Section 5. |

## 3. Honest uncertainty — what the MVP does NOT yet defend

Per the covenant seed, verbatim: **"Prefer honest uncertainty over fabrication."** The following are open surfaces in the v0 sim/MVP. Listing them is a covenant obligation, not a weakness disclosure to be minimized.

- **Real PoST physics (until Phase 6).** The MVP body is abstract space units and stub slots. Plots do not yet answer real proof-of-space challenges on a Chia-family research fork with header extensions; `plot_challenge_proof` in the header is simulated. Until Phase 6, I2 (`plot_challenge_honesty`) measures a sim, and the physical cost of faking space is not yet enforced by physics ([ARCHITECTURE.md](ARCHITECTURE.md)).
- **Real VDF clock.** There is no timelord. Slots are stub slots (`SLOTS_PER_EPOCH = 32`, `GENESIS_SLOT = 0`; `GENESIS_TIMESTAMP = "2026-01-01T00:00:00Z"` is a fixed label — consensus uses slots). Time-based bounds (`UNBOND_DELAY_SLOTS`, `ACTIVATION_DELAY_SLOTS`, `VOTING_WINDOW_SLOTS`, `MAX_CHALLENGE_GAP_SLOTS`) are only as strong as the sim clock until VDF physics arrives with the Phase 6 fork.
- **Network-level DoS and peering-layer eclipse.** The K11 witness rule addresses head attestation, not packets. Bandwidth exhaustion, peer-table poisoning, and transport-layer isolation of a node are out of scope for the sim and undefended in the MVP.
- **Key management.** How farmers, councilors, and witnesses generate, store, rotate, and recover their signing keys is outside the protocol. A stolen councilor key is indistinguishable from that councilor; the protocol bounds the damage (bonds, thresholds, G16) but cannot prevent the theft.
- **Post-quantum signatures.** The header reserves the field (`pq_reserved`, "post-quantum field reserved, null in MVP") but no PQ scheme is active. Migration is a MAJOR change.
- **Economics under real markets.** Reward, halving, and deposit values (`BASE_REWARD_PER_SLOT_CHRONONS` = 64 Chronos, `HALVING_INTERVAL_SLOTS` = 2^20, `COMMUNITY_PROPOSAL_DEPOSIT_CHRONONS` = 100 Chronos) are sim values; the mainnet schedule is explicitly M4. Whether these bounds actually price out attacks against real adversary budgets is untested.
- **Sybil formation of the witness and council sets.** Floors gate eligibility, but the MVP has no defense against one wealthy actor legitimately funding many bonded identities beyond the cost of the bonds themselves.

None of these gaps is patched by trust in Chronarch, the Council, or any operator. They are open until closed by engineering plus Proposal + Ballot — never by an assurance.

## 4. Above-threshold governance risk, stated plainly

A colluding Council supermajority that stays **within** G1–G13 legality is the hardest adversary in this model. G16 slashes illegal votes; it does not slash legal-but-harmful ones. The bounds that remain are structural: both approval thresholds are measured against eligible totals (abstention counts against, never for); every approval waits `ACTIVATION_DELAY_SLOTS = 32` slots in public before activating (`VOTING_WINDOW_SLOTS = 128` before that); the covenant itself is out of reach without a hard fork (G7); Challenge outcomes remain unbuyable (G2); and each colluder keeps ≥ 1000 Chronos of slashable bond exposed to any G16 boundary they misjudge. Verbatim from `SLOGANS["change"]` — an invariant encoded in [GENESIS.md](GENESIS.md) and [COUNCIL.md](COUNCIL.md): **"Major change is a proposal ring plus a slashing-backed vote, not an AI rewrite and not an admin key."** The last resort against a captured-but-legal Council is the same as in any covenant system: the hard fork, taken in the open, with the full scar record intact on both sides of the split (G1, G5).
